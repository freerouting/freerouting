## Severity guidance

- **Critical:** unauthenticated RCE, broad auth bypass, cloud-metadata SSRF, or equivalent.
- **High:** cross-user job/session access, secret exposure, or unintended MCP authority.
- **Medium:** meaningful DoS, missing limits on expensive routes, or exploitable information leak.
- **Low:** defense-in-depth or narrow operational weakness.
- **Informational:** documentation, hardening, or accepted design risk without a demonstrated exploit.
